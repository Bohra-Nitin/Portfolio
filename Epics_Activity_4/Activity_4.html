Drag & Drop — Peer Review Feedback

The six comments are mixed. Sort them into three categories: 2 KEEP + 2 QUESTION + 2 CHANGE / TEST. Drag a card, or click a card and then click a category. Use the question in each category as your hint.

“The prototype clearly shows the intended interaction; users understood it in the trial.”
“What evidence supports the assumption that users will maintain it?”
“Could you test the opening at a second height after observing the reach problem?”
“The test record clearly links the result to the requirement.”
“What happens when the prototype is used under the community site's conditions?”
“Try a different handle position and compare task completion time.”
