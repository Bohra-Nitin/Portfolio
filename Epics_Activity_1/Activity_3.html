Think Before You Build

Practise observation, assumptions and simple evidence-based decisions.

Question 1 — What is an observation?

CASE:

During a 10-minute observation, a team sees 25 students waiting near a bus stop.

Question 2 — What is an assumption?

CASE:

A bin is overflowing at 1:00 PM.

Question 3 — What information is missing?

CASE:

A government-school classroom feels uncomfortable during hot afternoons.

Question 4 — Which is the strongest first step?

CASE:

Residents report that water is sometimes wasted from an overhead tank. The team does not yet know when it happens or why.

Final Basic Scenario 🏁

A LOCAL LIBRARY PROBLEM:

Students say the library becomes crowded in the evening. One team member says, “Let us build a seat-booking app.” What is the most reasonable response?
