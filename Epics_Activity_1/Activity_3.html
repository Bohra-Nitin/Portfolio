ACTIVITY 3 • ABOUT 40–50 MINUTES

Think Before You Build

Practise observation, assumptions, missing information, evidence and simple engineering decisions before selecting a solution.

Core idea: A quick observation can tell you what happened. It does not automatically tell you why it happened or what should be built.

Question 1 — What is an observation?

CASE

During a 10-minute observation, a team sees 25 students waiting near a bus stop.

Question 2 — What is an assumption?

CASE

A waste bin is overflowing at 1:00 PM. The team has not yet studied how often the bin fills or what happens before it overflows.

Question 3 — What information is missing?

CASE

A government-school classroom feels uncomfortable during hot afternoons. The team has not measured the indoor conditions.

Question 4 — What should happen first?

CASE

Residents report that water is sometimes wasted from an overhead tank. The team does not yet know when the loss occurs or what causes it.

Scenario 1 — Do not confuse a symptom with a cause

CASE

Students report that a campus drinking-water point often has a queue. A team concludes that “the water dispenser is too slow” after seeing a queue once.
