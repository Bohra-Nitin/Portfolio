People & Community

Explore stakeholders, community needs, listening, observation, inclusion, and ethical engagement before deciding what an engineering project should do.

Core idea: A community problem is experienced by people. Different people may see different parts of the same problem.

Choose the definition that is broad enough for an engineering project.

Question 2 — Whose perspective should you hear?

SCENARIO

A school wants to improve access to drinking water. Students use the water points every day, teachers supervise the school, maintenance staff repair the plumbing, and management controls spending and approval.

Question 3 — Primary or supporting stakeholder?

A maintenance worker will regularly inspect and repair a proposed community water-level indicator.

Question 4 — Which interview question is most useful?

SCENARIO
